# real_robot_conds.txt: conditions that model the student's real robot (sim/real_robot_test.sh reads this).
#
# Every condition comes from the log of the 19 real runs (real_runs_log.txt; the analysis is in the
# forensics NOTES: r16/forensics/NOTES.md and runs.md). "run N" below is the N-th "MISSION: go" of that log.
#
# Format, one condition per line:
#   name | routes | sim flags | why (from the log)
# routes: 312 (the order the student uses), 132, or 312,132 (both: where the route changes what happens).
# @NAME = flags   defines a short name; $NAME in a line is replaced by it.
# Lines starting with # are comments.
#
# REALM: the robot fitted to the real runs (forensics fit5.tsv): the old firmware in the sim then turns
# L90 635 / R90 617 / L180 1160 ms and cruises at vScale 1.76-1.81, the real robot L90 650 / R90 563 /
# L180 1100 ms and 1.64-1.81. Its first crossing comes at d=8.0 with the wheels 5 cm past C1, the real
# runs at d=6.6-8.9: so the real placement is --startx=5 (the sim's x is the axle, C1 = 0).
@REALM = --student --vmax=65 --tau=90 --scrub=0.05 --dbL=38 --dbR=24 --trimL=0.80 --trimLr=0.76
@X = --startx=5
# The real white levels at the start of runs 4, 5 and 16 (A0..A7 over white, the line read ~860-960):
@TILT4  = --chan=0:651:960 --chan=1:456:960 --chan=2:400:960 --chan=3:360:960 --chan=4:336:960 --chan=5:249:960 --chan=6:216:960 --chan=7:208:960
@TILT5  = --chan=0:656:960 --chan=1:428:960 --chan=2:330:960 --chan=3:280:960 --chan=4:242:960 --chan=5:157:960 --chan=6:149:960 --chan=7:130:960
@TILT16 = --chan=0:625:960 --chan=1:194:960 --chan=2:133:960 --chan=3:120:960 --chan=4:110:960 --chan=5:211:960 --chan=6:146:960 --chan=7:80:960

# ---- the robot itself
realm        | 312,132 | $REALM $X              | the fit to the 19 runs: turn times, cruise speed and the first crossing at d=6.6-8.9 all match
student      | 312,132 | --student $X           | the --student preset (best estimate before the log): a second model of the same robot
student-slow | 312,132 | --student-slow $X      | the slow end of the preset (tired PP3, slower motors, bigger dead bands)
student-fast | 312,132 | --student-fast $X      | the fast end of the preset (fresh PP3); real cruise vScale reached 2.3-2.5 in runs 5 and 15

# ---- where the student puts the robot down
place-x-4    | 312,132 | $REALM --startx=-4     | wheels 4 cm before C1 (the bar 5.5 cm past C1): the near end of the -4..8 cm a student may put it down in
place-x0     | 312,132 | $REALM --startx=0      | wheels over C1 (what e33_mission asks for); run 4's n=1 at d=11.6 means about 1.5 cm past C1
place-x8     | 312,132 | $REALM --startx=8      | wheels 8 cm past C1: a little beyond the far end seen (run 10's n=1 at d=6.6 means about 6.5 cm past C1)

# ---- the sensor bar
a0-stuck     | 312,132 | $REALM $X --chan=0:640:950 | A0 reads ~590-670 on white from run 3 on (it still rises on black): a stuck-high left sensor
a0-drift     | 312     | $REALM $X --wshift=440:0:6:01 | run 3: A0 drifted 176 -> 615 while it stood at the start, then FAULT 6 at n=3 with bar 10000000 (A0 stuck on)
tilt-run4    | 312,132 | $REALM $X $TILT4       | run 4's whites {651,456,...,208}: the bar tilted, left end high; runs 4,7,8,10 had A0 lit on crossings
tilt-run5    | 312     | $REALM $X $TILT5       | run 5's whites {656,428,...,130} (FAULT 2: on a crossing too long, bar 01111111)
tilt-run16   | 312     | $REALM $X $TILT16      | run 16's whites {625,194,...,80} (FAULT 2 at n=5, masks 00111111)

# ---- the false crossing after a standing start (--lift)
lift         | 312,132 | $REALM $X --lift=0.6   | false "crossings" 1.4-2.3 cm after the start and 1.3-4.6 cm after turns; G 0.6 reproduced run 1 (n=7 turn at d=2.0 after the 180, then a FAULT at n=9)
lift-strong  | 312,132 | $REALM $X --lift=0.8:150 | the same, stronger and longer (the other fit of run 1); real d of the false counts up to 4.6 cm
lift-tilt4   | 312     | $REALM $X $TILT4 --lift=0.6 | run 4's start as a whole: tilted whites and the false crossing (real masks 00111111/00011111)

# ---- the Nano restarting in the middle of a run (--reset; the real restarts read "power-on")
rst-turn1    | 312,132 | $REALM $X --reset=spin:1:200      | runs 7, 10, 12: restart in the FIRST turn (no servo moving: motor start current on a weak PP3)
rst-pick1    | 312,132 | $REALM $X --reset=grip:1:150      | run 4: PICK at n=5 (VCC min 4.52 V), then "RESTARTED here"
rst-pick180  | 312,132 | $REALM $X --reset=pickturn:1:300  | runs 4 and 11: restart in the 180 after the pick (gap-turn rescue printed, L180 2052 ms)
rst-place1   | 312,132 | $REALM $X --reset=release:1:100   | run 6: the place at n=12 pulled VCC to 4.48 V (LOW) and the Nano restarted 4 counts later; a place moves the same servos as a pick
rst-twice    | 312     | $REALM $X --reset=grip:1:150 --reset=pickturn:2:300 | a weak PP3 restarts at more than one pick (runs 4, 6, 11 each restarted at a pick or place)

# ---- the battery (one PP3 for motors, Nano and servos)
tired        | 312,132 | $REALM $X --vmax=44 --vbat=6.6 --rint=3.5 --bor=restart --maxresets=2 | runs 12 and 14: rest 4.83 V (a flat PP3), cruise vScale 1.16 (not 1.7); run 12 restarted at its first turn
bor-servo    | 312,132 | $REALM $X --vbat=7.2 --rint=4 --bor=restart --maxresets=2 | a weak PP3 (4 ohm): a servo moving while the gripper holds an object pulls the 5 V under the reset level (2.7 V), so every pick restarts the Nano (runs 4, 11: VCC min 4.52/4.54 V at the pick, then the restart)
